Writing and insights on the future of education in the age of AI.

Kolot — Journal of Education and Society | Issue 32 | June 2026

"I was always told that without an education, I wouldn't get a good profession; but what profession will I have if I do?"

Merav Assaf & Idan Shavit

Written and published in Hebrew for Kolot, a journal of education and society. The article argues that the traditional bargain driving education — study hard, secure a good profession — has broken down, and proposes what schools need to change to stay meaningful in the age of AI: cognitive health education, fostering an internal values compass, and creating space to practice soft skills.
