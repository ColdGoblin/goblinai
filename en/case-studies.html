Case Studies

Real-World AI Education

Examples of AI training and consultancy delivered for schools, colleges, organisations, and communities.

Teacher Training Workshop — Kaye College

A custom 9-workshop series for Kaye College lecturers, from AI basics and digital content creation to NotebookLM, AI chatbots for teaching, and Google Studio integration.

Challenge: Teachers needed practical AI skills but lacked hands-on experience with educational tools.
Approach: Nine practical workshops tailored to Kaye College lecturers.
Result: Over 60 lecturers trained across the series.

VOLCANIC Project — EU Erasmus+

Cross-European collaboration on inclusive AI strategies for disadvantaged populations.

Challenge: Educational institutions across Europe needed inclusive AI strategies for disadvantaged populations.
Approach: International Erasmus+ collaboration on inclusive practice.
Result: A shared inclusive-AI framework across 12 institutions in 7 countries.

Community AI Literacy Programme

Making AI approachable for older adults who felt left behind by rapidly advancing technology.

Challenge: Older adults felt left behind by rapidly advancing AI technology.
Approach: Patient, accessible community-based AI literacy sessions.
Result: A running community of over 300 participants.

Anne's Family Diary Project

Helping a family preserve and digitise their ancestor's diary from generations past using AI tools.

Challenge: A family wanted to preserve and digitise their ancestor's diary from generations past.
Approach: AI-assisted transcription and digitisation.
Result: A preserved, accessible family record for future generations.

Daybreak — Senior Staff AI Training

Ongoing volunteer training for senior staff, helping them save time and energy by streamlining admin and office tasks with AI.

Challenge: Senior staff time consumed by repetitive admin and office tasks.
Approach: Ongoing volunteer, hands-on AI training focused on real workflows.
Result: 4 senior staff trained so far — still early days, with more impact expected as the programme grows.

OUNC — AI for Everyday Life

Workshops on using AI to streamline daily life, including job search and CV writing.

Challenge: Participants needed practical AI skills for job hunting and daily productivity.
Approach: Hands-on workshops covering CV writing, job search, and everyday AI use.
Result: Over 50 participants trained across all sessions.

Oxford OJC — Public AI Workshops

Public workshops on AI best practices and the use of AI in teaching.

Challenge: Community members wanted accessible guidance on responsible AI use and its role in teaching.
Approach: Public workshops covering best practices and classroom applications.
Result: 10 workshops delivered so far, on an ongoing basis.
