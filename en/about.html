About

Idan Shavit

Educator, lecturer, and AI-integration specialist based in Oxford East. Over two decades of experience in non-formal education across Israel and Europe — from Kibbutz Sde Boker to Sofia, Bulgaria, and Oxford.

Oxford — where centuries of scholarship meet the tools of the AI era

Core Philosophy: AI Meets Guided Autonomy

Non-formal education fosters guided autonomous learning — empowering learners to develop self-directed skills while receiving structured support. AI amplifies this approach by personalising learning paths, automating routine tasks, and freeing educators to focus on creativity, critical thinking, and ethical awareness. Educators should embrace AI as a tool for autonomy, not fear it as a replacement.

My Journey

I've always been drawn to computers and technology. During COVID, like the rest of the education system, we had to find new ways to teach remotely — using technology to bridge the gap that had opened up. That's where I started integrating more and more techno-pedagogy into my work. And when generative AI reached the public, I was fascinated by its possibilities from the very start — and I jumped in without a second thought.

That journey took me from teaching non-formal education and pedagogical mentoring at Kaye College's Democratic Institute, to advising the college's IT department, and on to the Innovation Team at Israel's Ministry of Education. Today I continue that journey leading a course at the Mofet Institute, guiding educators to discover what AI makes possible in teaching and learning.
